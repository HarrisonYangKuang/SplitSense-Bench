## Human/AI attribution limits

The public package does not contain a complete, independently audited history assigning each original line or decision to a human or AI tool. It therefore does not support a quantified claim of sole human implementation. AI assistance must be disclosed when presenting this project; a personal statement should describe the author's actual work and decisions rather than infer them from a repository author field.
